📌 1. Executive Summary

EventHub AI solves four systemic disconnects in the university and hackathon ecosystem: information buried in long PDF notices, students having creative event ideas with no formal approval channel, fragmented student portfolios, and weeks-long delays issuing certificates.

Powered by Cloudinary Programmable Media Layer Overlays, tickets, badges, and certificates are edge-rendered dynamically via URLs without heavy server rendering load.

🏆 2. Problem Statement & Solution Breakdown (50% Evaluation)

Campus Touchpoint The Broken Reality The EventHub AI Solution
Notice Dissemination Messy 5-page PDF circulars. Students miss deadlines and eligibility. Circular2Action LLM: Extracts dates, prize money, team sizes, and generates a live registration form in seconds.
Student Ideation Students have creative event ideas but no pipeline to propose them to faculty. Idea Incubator: Propose ideas; AI scores feasibility (1-100) and drafts a budget; faculty can approve and 1-click launch live events.
Ticketing & RSVP Fragile Google forms with no confirmation or digital badges. Dynamic Ticketing: Clear pricing tiers, promo codes (HACKTOBER50), and Cloudinary admission tickets with barcodes.
Short Courses Lack of essential topics in traditional syllabus (Finance, Cyber, Rust). Online Lectures: Live streams and On-Demand masterclasses streamed with Cloudinary video optimization.
Hackathon Collab Teams work across disconnected tabs and suffer painful last-minute merges. TeamForge Studio: Simultaneous Frontend, Backend & DB schema workspace with 1-click synchronized release merge!
Developer Mentorship Beginners copy-paste errors without understanding why they happen. ErrorLens AI: Explains root cause, points out faulty lines, offers hints, and teaches the fix pedagogically.

☁️ 3. Cloudinary Integration Matrix (30% Evaluation)

EventHub uses Cloudinary deep into its functional workflow:

  • Dynamic Edge Certificates: Multi-layer text overlays (l_text:Playfair Display, Cinzel, Roboto) with zero server-side Chromium/Puppeteer overhead.
  • Dynamic Admission Tickets: Overlays attendee name, event title, seat tier, ticket ID, and barcode directly onto ticket passes.
  • Milestone Badges: Dynamic open-source circular badges (First PR Merged, Git Master).
  • AI Auto-Cropping (g_auto): Responsive 16:9, 4:3, and 9:16 aspect ratios.
  • Adaptive Video Streaming: Optimized playback (vc_auto,q_auto,w_1280).
